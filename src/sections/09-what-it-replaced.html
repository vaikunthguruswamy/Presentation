09

What it replaced, task by task

The platform is easier to judge one process at a time. In each case the old method still worked. It just consumed skilled people's days.

Process
Before
Now
Management reporting
Manual exports rebuilt into Excel charts each time a figure was needed
Live dashboards across all three divisions, over 100 graphs, no export step
Payroll verification
Roughly a week, several people cross-checking every clock-in by hand
Generated in a few clicks, with anomalies flagged automatically
Invoice tallying
Days of reading PDFs and reconciling against the system by hand
Batch upload, extraction and cross-check against stock in minutes
Component matching
Hours per bill of materials, switching between supplier sites row by row
One upload, ranked matches from stock and suppliers on a single screen
Customer order updates
Staff emailing each customer with the day's status
Customers see their own live position and delivery performance themselves
PCB job cards
Hundreds of printed sheets a day, untraceable once filed
Signed on a tablet, full paper trail per board, searchable months later
Recruitment screening
Every CV read by hand; applications arriving by email
Anonymised scoring with a written analysis, inside a hiring pipeline
Water use
Meters displayed a reading and stored nothing
Logged continuously to the ERP with high-flow alerting
