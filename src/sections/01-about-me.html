A little about me

I studied electronics for my undergraduate degree and artificial intelligence for my master's. ZOT is my first full-time job in the United Kingdom. I joined in February 2025 as an intern through the company's placement partnership with Bright Green Business, and I am still the only software engineer here.

The electronics background matters more than it might sound. It means I can sit down with a process engineer and understand what a layer, an etch bath or an impedance reading actually is, before trying to put it in a database. Most of my work has started as a conversation on the factory floor rather than a specification handed to me.

I am conscious that I have been here eighteen months, and that some of the people I work alongside have been in electronics manufacturing for thirty years. What I have tried to contribute is the part they did not have in the building: someone whose whole job is to notice where the work is being made harder than it needs to be, and then fix it.
