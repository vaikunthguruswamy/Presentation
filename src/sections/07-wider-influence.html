Wider influence

Outside the business, ZOT encouraged me to attend research conferences and technical meetups, where I could learn from engineers with far more experience than me. My placement was published as a case study by the Midlothian and East Lothian Chamber of Commerce, and I am glad it is being used to make the case for taking on technical interns in manufacturing.

We regularly host groups of students from the University of Edinburgh, Edinburgh Napier University and Heriot-Watt University on the shop floor. I walk them through our development progress and our transition into Industry 4.0 with complete traceability from order to dispatch, and talk openly about the kind of opportunity a fresh graduate can find in manufacturing.
