Where this sits in the sector

UK manufacturing is not short of enthusiasm for digital technology. It is short of three specific things: the skills to implement it, the capital to buy it, and systems old enough to make integration the hard part. Published sector research puts numbers on all three.

60%

of manufacturers say skills are the major barrier to adopting AI and automation.

Made Smarter SME market research

ZOT had exactly that profile: keen, cash-disciplined, and running production on systems that pre-dated the cloud. The route we took was to build the capability inside the business rather than buy it: one engineer, open-source and cloud consumption costs, and no licence per seat. That is a route more SMEs can afford than the sector's spending figures suggest, and it is the part of this story I think generalises.
